Who We Are

Built by the Community, for the Community

Pittsburgh Ruff Ryders is an all-volunteer 501(c)(3) nonprofit based in Pittsburgh. We serve the general public across Allegheny County, working alongside neighbors who need food, warm supplies, and access to care.

Every program is run by volunteers and delivered with partners already trusted in the neighborhoods we serve. No paid staff, no membership requirement, no cost to participate.

Pittsburgh Ruff Ryders Inc. is recognized by the IRS as a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt charitable organization. Our programs are open to the general public regardless of race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, veteran status, sexual orientation, or gender identity.

Programs

What We Do

Four programs, run year-round by volunteers and delivered with local partners.

01

Homeless Feeding Initiative

Hot meals and grocery distribution for unsheltered neighbors, delivered with the Greater Pittsburgh Community Food Bank and Light of Life Rescue Mission.

02

Winter Care Packages

Coats, hats, gloves, socks, and hygiene supplies assembled and handed out directly during the coldest months of the year.

03

Preventive Health Access

Free screenings and connections to ongoing care through UPMC and Central Outreach Wellness Center, brought to community events instead of waiting rooms.

04

Youth & Family Recreation

Safe, free recreational gatherings that give Pittsburgh youth and families somewhere to be together, on the road and in the neighborhood.

Privacy

Privacy Policy

What we collect

If you email us, donate, or sign up to volunteer, we collect the name, email address, and any other details you choose to provide. Donations are handled by a third-party payment processor; we never see or store your card number.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to you, process and acknowledge donations, coordinate volunteer work, and send occasional updates about our programs. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

Analytics and cookies

This site uses standard web analytics to understand how visitors find and use our pages. Analytics data is aggregated and is not used to identify individual visitors. You can block cookies in your browser without losing access to any part of this site.

Your choices

Write to stogie@pittsburghrr.org at any time to request a copy of what we hold about you, correct it, unsubscribe, or have it deleted. This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.
